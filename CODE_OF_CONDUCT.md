# Our Commitment

At **Chasha**, we believe that exceptional restaurant technology is built by exceptional engineering teams.

We are committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment for everyone contributing to the Chasha platform — including engineers, designers, product managers, maintainers, contractors, and collaborators.

Our engineering culture is founded on:

- Professionalism
- Respect
- Collaboration
- Innovation
- Ownership
- Knowledge Sharing
- Constructive Feedback
- Continuous Improvement

Everyone participating in this project is expected to uphold these values.

---

# Our Philosophy

Chasha is more than a restaurant management system.

Chasha is building the next generation operating platform for modern restaurants — helping restaurants operate faster, smarter, and more efficiently.

Achieving this vision requires:

- Mutual Respect
- Engineering Excellence
- Product Thinking
- Intellectual Honesty
- Open Communication
- Shared Ownership
- Customer-Centric Decisions
- Continuous Learning

We believe that engineering culture is as important as software architecture.

---

# Expected Behavior

## Professional Communication

Contributors are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Welcome constructive feedback.
- Share ideas objectively.
- Encourage healthy technical discussions.
- Focus on solving problems rather than assigning blame.
- Respect different perspectives and experiences.

---

## Collaboration

Contributors should:

- Help teammates succeed.
- Share technical knowledge openly.
- Encourage mentorship and learning.
- Support collaborative problem solving.
- Prioritize team success over individual recognition.
- Communicate blockers early.

---

## Technical Discussions

Technical disagreements are expected and encouraged when handled professionally.

Contributors should:

- Challenge ideas, not people.
- Use technical reasoning and evidence.
- Remain open to alternative approaches.
- Prioritize maintainability and business value.
- Respect architectural decisions.
- Think about long-term impact.

---

## Professional Responsibility

Every contributor is expected to:

- Follow Chasha architectural principles.
- Respect security requirements.
- Maintain code quality standards.
- Write clean and maintainable software.
- Document important decisions.
- Consider scalability and long-term sustainability.
- Protect customer and restaurant data.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable.

---

## Harassment

Including but not limited to:

- Personal attacks
- Insults
- Threats
- Intimidation
- Harassment
- Discriminatory language
- Hate speech
- Offensive behavior

---

## Unprofessional Conduct

Examples include:

- Deliberately disruptive behavior
- Bad-faith arguments
- Repeated hostility
- Refusal to collaborate
- Toxic communication
- Public shaming
- Intentional misinformation

---

## Engineering Misconduct

Examples include:

- Intentionally introducing vulnerabilities
- Bypassing security controls
- Ignoring architectural standards
- Introducing malicious code
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information
- Misrepresenting technical work
- Circumventing review processes

---

# Engineering Culture Principles

Chasha engineering culture is based on the following principles.

---

## Ownership

Every contributor owns the quality, reliability, and maintainability of their work.

---

## Accountability

Every technical decision has consequences.

We build with responsibility and intention.

---

## Transparency

Important decisions should be documented and communicated clearly.

---

## Simplicity

Prefer simple and maintainable solutions over unnecessary complexity.

---

## Customer First

Every engineering decision should improve the experience of restaurants, staff, and customers.

---

## Security First

Security is never optional.

Customer data, restaurant information, and platform integrity must always be protected.

---

## Long-Term Thinking

Short-term solutions should not create long-term technical debt.

---

# Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately.

## Contact

```text
conduct@chasha.app
```

Reports should include:

- Description of the incident
- Individuals involved
- Relevant context
- Supporting evidence (if available)

All reports will be reviewed confidentially.

---

# Enforcement Policy

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Possible enforcement actions include:

- Warning
- Temporary restriction
- Removal of privileges
- Suspension of access
- Permanent exclusion from the project

The severity of enforcement depends on the nature and impact of the violation.
